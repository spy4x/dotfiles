# Music for the dotfiles demo video

`music.opus` is the music the dotfiles demo video's encoder lays under the video.

- Title: cold-morning-master
- Author: John Bartmann
- Album: 100 Ambient Atmospheric Soundtracks: Straylight Drones Collection (2021)
- Source:
  https://freemusicarchive.org/music/John_Bartmann/100-ambient-atmospheric-soundtracks-straylight-drones-collection/cold-morning-master
- Copy downloaded from Wikimedia Commons:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Bartmann_-_cold-morning-master.ogg
  (file: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/6c/John_Bartmann_-_cold-morning-master.ogg)
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal, a public domain dedication
  (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Both pages above state it. It allows
  commercial use with no attribution; this file names the source anyway.

The file here is 90 seconds of the track starting at 0:20, where it is already steady, re-encoded
to Opus at 96 kbit/s, stereo, with all metadata removed and no other change. The video's encoder trims
it to the video's length, sets its level, and fades it in and out.
